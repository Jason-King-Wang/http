Huoke — Product Marketing & Lead Generation

A workflow in progress that uses shared product facts across website content, customer questions, social activity and lead follow-up.

  • Node.js
  • SQLite
  • Content workflows
  • Retrieval
  • Social integration

One product, consistent information

A product introduction, an FAQ answer and a social post should agree on what a product does. Huoke explores how these activities can work from the same product facts rather than accumulating separate, inconsistent descriptions.

The project covers application development and integration around that workflow. Several modules have been implemented; end-to-end integration is in progress.

Modules developed so far

  • Content for landing pages, articles, FAQs, short-video scripts, email drafts and social-card assets.
  • Editing, preview and static website export using supplied product information.
  • FAQ and lexical-retrieval chat prototypes, with a path for questions that need further information.
  • Content rotation, Threads discovery and reply-draft workflows.
  • Consent-based lead capture and follow-up stages.

Managing social activity

The social modules include relevance screening, duplicate detection, cooldown periods and usage limits. An operation with an unconfirmed result needs review before it is repeated.

These controls are part of the implementation work. Actual platform access, permissions and live behavior remain separate integration requirements.

The intended flow

  1. Product facts establish what can be said.
  2. Content and website pages explain the offer and use cases.
  3. Questions and social interactions provide context for a visitor's interest.
  4. Consented inquiries enter a follow-up process.

Current stage

In progress

Modules have been implemented. Current work focuses on integration and live-platform validation. Local prototypes do not establish customer acquisition results or revenue. This page presents a development project and its current scope.
